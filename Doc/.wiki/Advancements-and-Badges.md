# Advancements & Badges

## Overview

Stack Size Adjuster relies on vanilla Minecraft advancement systems and intentionally omits custom advancement JSON triggers.

---

## 🎖️ Mechanics & Vanilla Parity

Under the **Absence Policy**, Stack Size Adjuster does not introduce custom advancement trees. Items modified by Stack Size Adjuster continue to trigger standard vanilla advancements:

* **Item Pickup Criteria**: Picking up large stacks of items triggers vanilla item criteria (e.g. `minecraft:inventory_changed`).
* **Crafting & Smelting**: Crafting or smelting large item stacks triggers vanilla recipe unlock advancements without interruption.
